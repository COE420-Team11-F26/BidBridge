# BidBridge Risk Register

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Possible Cause | Probability | Impact | Mitigation / Response Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | **Changing or unclear requirements** could cause us to change parts of the system after we already started developing them. | Some details about how bidding, job approval, or payment should work may not be completely clear at the beginning. | Medium | Medium | We will agree on the main requirements before starting each feature and discuss any changes with the team before implementing them. |
| **R2** | **Technical difficulties** may happen when implementing the payment and escrow feature. | Our team may face problems connecting the system to a payment service or understanding how the payment process should work. | Medium | High | We will research the payment feature early and test it separately before connecting it to the rest of BidBridge. If needed, we can use a test or simulated payment system for the project. |
| **R3** | **Team member availability** may affect the progress of the project. | Team members may be busy with other courses, exams, assignments, or personal commitments. | Medium | Medium | We will divide the work early, give each member clear tasks, and regularly check our progress so unfinished work can be reassigned if needed. |
| **R4** | **Integration problems** may happen when combining the different parts of BidBridge. | Different team members may work on the frontend, backend, database, bidding system, and payment system separately. | Medium | High | We will use Git branches for separate features and merge our work regularly instead of waiting until the end. We will also test the system after combining major features. |
| **R5** | **Security or privacy concerns** could affect user accounts, bids, or payment-related information. | Incorrect access control, weak password handling, or mistakes in how user data is stored could create security problems. | Medium | High | We will use proper login and access control, validate user input, store passwords securely, and test that users can only access information they are allowed to see. |
