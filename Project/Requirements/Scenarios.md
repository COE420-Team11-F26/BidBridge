\## Exercise 1: Scenario Identification



| Scenario ID | Scenario Title | Actor/Stakeholder | Scenario Description |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| S-01 | Client posts a design job | Client | Sara, a café owner, logs in and posts "Logo for specialty coffee shop" with required skill Adobe Illustrator, budget AED 500–800, and a 10-day deadline. The job appears in the public listing with status Open. |

| S-02 | Freelancer bids on a job | Freelancer | Omar filters open jobs by the skill "Illustrator", opens Sara's job, and submits a bid of AED 650 with a 7-day delivery time and a link to his portfolio. Sara is notified that a new bid was received. |

| S-03 | Client accepts a bid and funds escrow | Client, Freelancer | Sara compares 6 bids by price, delivery time, and rating, then accepts Omar's bid. The system holds AED 650 in escrow, changes the job to Assigned, notifies Omar, and informs the other 5 bidders that the job is closed. |

| S-04 | Work submitted, approved, and paid | Freelancer, Client | Omar uploads logo\_final.zip with a note. The job becomes Submitted. Sara first requests a revision ("use a darker brown"); Omar resubmits, Sara approves, the AED 650 is released to Omar's balance, the job becomes Completed, and both parties leave a rating. |

| S-05 | Dispute over a late delivery | Client, Platform Administrator | A client's website job passes its deadline by 5 days with no submission. The client opens a dispute; the escrow is frozen. The administrator reviews the job history and messages, then refunds the escrowed amount to the client and closes the job. |
