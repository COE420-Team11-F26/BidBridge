# BidBridge — Core Processes Matrix

The processes, inputs and outputs below are the same as the Level-0 DFD (`Level_0_DFD/BidBridge_Level0_DFD.png`). The source of each input and the destination of each output is shown in brackets. FR references: (E) Emran, (M) Mohamed; FR-11 to FR-20 are unique numbers.

| Process No. and Name | Input Data | Output Data | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P1 – Manage User Accounts** | • Registration, login & profile details [Client]<br>• Registration, login & portfolio details [Freelancer]<br>• Admin login credentials [Administrator]<br>• Stored credentials & profile [D1 Users] | • Login result & account status [Client, Freelancer]<br>• Login result [Administrator]<br>• Account data [D1 Users] | 1. Registration requires an email address, a password and a role of Client or Freelancer (FR-03 M).<br>2. Login succeeds only if the email and password match the record in D1; otherwise the attempt is rejected and an error message is returned (FR-11).<br>3. A suspended account cannot log in until an administrator reactivates it (FR-19).<br>4. All users can update their profile; only freelancers can add skills, a biography and portfolio information (FR-15). |
| **P2 – Manage Job Postings** | • Job posting details [Client]<br>• Job search keywords [Freelancer]<br>• Open job listings [D2 Jobs] | • Matching open jobs [Freelancer]<br>• New job posting [D2 Jobs] | 1. Only a logged-in client can create a posting (R-04), and it must include a title, description, required skills and a bid deadline (FR-01 M, UC-17).<br>2. A new posting is saved with status Open.<br>3. A search matches keywords against the task title and required skills (FR-04 M).<br>4. Search results only include jobs that are Open, still accepting bids and not hidden by an administrator (FR-17, FR-19). |
| **P3 – Process Bids** | • Bid proposal / edit / withdrawal [Freelancer]<br>• Bid acceptance [Client]<br>• Job status & bid deadline [D2 Jobs]<br>• Job bids [D3 Bids]<br>• Freelancer ratings [D4 Job Activity] | • New-bid notifications & sorted bid list [Client]<br>• Bid status, history & job assignment [Freelancer]<br>• Bid record [D3 Bids]<br>• Accepted bid [D2 Jobs] | 1. Only a logged-in freelancer can bid (R-05). A bid must include a price, completion time and information about the freelancer (FR-02 M).<br>2. Bids are accepted only while the job is Open and before its deadline. If the deadline passes with no accepted bid, the job stops accepting bids (FR-17).<br>3. A bid can be edited or withdrawn only while the job is Open and the bid has not been accepted (FR-13).<br>4. Each new bid notifies the job's client with the job title, freelancer name, bid price and submission time (FR-16).<br>5. The client can sort bids by price, delivery time or freelancer rating (FR-12).<br>6. The client accepts exactly one bid. It is recorded as the accepted bid, but the job only becomes Assigned once P5 confirms the escrow hold (FR-01 E).<br>7. A freelancer's bid history shows each bid's job, amount, delivery time and status (pending, accepted, rejected or withdrawn) (FR-18). |
| **P4 – Manage Work Delivery** | • Work submission (files & note) [Freelancer]<br>• Work approval / revision request [Client]<br>• Job messages & freelancer rating [Client]<br>• Job messages & client rating [Freelancer]<br>• Activity history [D4 Job Activity]<br>• Job details [D2 Jobs] | • Submitted work files & notification [Client]<br>• Freelancer messages [Client]<br>• Revision requests & client messages [Freelancer]<br>• Activity record [D4 Job Activity]<br>• Job status [D2 Jobs]<br>• Approved work (release request) [P5] | 1. Only the assigned freelancer of an Assigned job can submit work: 1–5 files of at most 25 MB each, plus a submission note. The job then becomes Submitted and the client is notified (FR-02 E).<br>2. The client of a Submitted job either approves the work or requests a revision. A revision request needs a written comment and returns the job to Assigned (FR-03 E).<br>3. Approval sends a release request to P5 (FR-04 E).<br>4. Messages can only be exchanged between the client and the assigned freelancer of an active job, and each message is stored with its sender and timestamp (FR-14).<br>5. Ratings (1–5 stars) can only be given after the job is completed, and the client and freelancer each rate the other once (FR-05 M). |
| **P5 – Handle Escrow Payments** | • Accepted bid & amount [D2 Jobs]<br>• Approved work (release request) [P4]<br>• Freeze / settlement order [P6]<br>• Escrow hold confirmation [Payment Service]<br>• Transfer confirmation [Payment Service]<br>• Escrow & payment details [D5 Transactions] | • Escrow hold request [Payment Service]<br>• Release / refund instruction [Payment Service]<br>• Job status (Assigned / Completed) [D2 Jobs]<br>• Transaction record [D5 Transactions]<br>• Transaction history & refund notice [Client]<br>• Payment release notice & transaction history [Freelancer] | 1. When a bid is accepted, P5 requests an escrow hold equal to the accepted bid price. The job changes from Open to Assigned only after the hold is confirmed (FR-01 E).<br>2. On a release request, the escrowed amount goes to the freelancer's platform balance, the job becomes Completed, and a transaction is recorded with the job ID, amount, payer, payee and timestamp (FR-04 E).<br>3. Escrow under an open dispute is frozen and cannot be released until a settlement order arrives from P6. The order either pays the freelancer or refunds the client (FR-05 E).<br>4. Users can only view their own transactions, each showing the job ID, amount, payment status, transaction type and timestamp (FR-20). |
| **P6 – Resolve Disputes** | • Dispute request [Client]<br>• Dispute request [Freelancer]<br>• Dispute resolution decision (pay / refund) [Administrator]<br>• Dispute details [D6 Disputes] | • Open dispute cases [Administrator]<br>• Dispute record [D6 Disputes]<br>• Freeze / settlement order [P5] | 1. Only the client or the assigned freelancer of a job can open a dispute, and only while the job is Assigned or Submitted. A reason is required (FR-05 E).<br>2. Opening a dispute immediately sends a freeze order to P5 and adds the case to the administrator's list of open disputes.<br>3. Only an administrator can resolve a dispute, and the resolution must be either pay the freelancer or refund the client. The decision is sent to P5 as a settlement order, and the dispute is closed (FR-05 E, R-03). |
| **P7 – Moderate Platform** | • Moderation action & reason [Administrator]<br>• Account details [D1 Users]<br>• Job posting details [D2 Jobs] | • Reported accounts & job postings [Administrator]<br>• Account status change [D1 Users]<br>• Posting visibility change [D2 Jobs] | 1. Only an administrator can suspend or reactivate accounts, and hide or remove job postings that break platform rules (FR-19).<br>2. Every moderation action must include a written reason, which is stored with the change (FR-19).<br>3. A hidden or removed posting no longer appears in search results (P2), and a suspended user can no longer log in (P1).<br>4. While moderating a posting, the administrator can also suspend the account that posted it (R-11). |
