BidBridge: Freelance job board with bidding and payment release

BidBridge is a freelance job platform that solves the problem that clients face in finding suitable freelancers, and freelancers face in finding job opportunities and a reliable payment. Clients would use BidBridge to post jobs and tasks, while freelancers can submit their competitive bids to secure that job. All while at the end the system provides a secure payment method and release upon successful completion of the task.
